First, a quick look at the categories. Across all three engines, these were the most recommended brands. In robot vacuums, Roborock led clearly, both in Turkey and globally. In coffee machines, Philips came first in Turkey and De'Longhi globally. Sneakers were a close race: Nike led in Turkey, and globally Nike and New Balance were tied. In perfume, Chanel and Dior tied in Turkey, while Chanel led globally. Lenovo topped gaming laptops in both markets, and LG topped TVs in both markets. But the really interesting part is how differently the engines behave behind these totals.
