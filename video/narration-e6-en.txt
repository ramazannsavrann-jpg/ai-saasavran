Results by question, and why you lost. Near the bottom of the report, every question is listed separately. You see where you were recommended, where you were only mentioned, and where you didn't come up at all. For questions you lost, a why you lost card opens. It lists, with evidence, who was recommended instead of you, which sites the AI relied on, and whether your own site was cited. Tap show answer to read the AI's full answer. The where you show up section groups questions by what the customer wants; for example, you may be strong with buyers but weak with people comparing options.
