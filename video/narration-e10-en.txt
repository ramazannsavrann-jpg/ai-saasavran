Share the report and put tracking on autopilot. Under the report tab, use share the report to create a link that expires after a set time. Anyone who opens the link sees the report without signing in, and can save it as a PDF. You can also download your measurements as a CSV to open in Excel. If measure automatically every week is on in your brand settings, your brand is measured each week on its own, and the next date is shown at the top of the page. On the account page, you can turn the weekly summary email and drop alerts on or off. That way, you can follow your AI visibility every week with no extra effort.
