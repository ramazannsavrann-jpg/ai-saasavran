First, the method. With AI SaaSavran, we wrote eight real buyer questions for each category. No brand names appear in the questions, just like a regular shopper would ask. English questions were asked from the US, and Turkish questions from Turkey. Whenever the AI looked at a website to answer, we recorded that link. Then we counted how many different answers cited each site. The categories are broad: phones, TVs, robot vacuums, coffee machines, smart rings, hospitals, hair transplant clinics, and even coffee shops in Seattle and moving companies in New York.
