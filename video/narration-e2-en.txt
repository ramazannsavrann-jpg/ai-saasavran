Create an account and add your brand. On A I Saa Savran dot com, tap create account. You can sign up with Google, or with an email and a password. First, create a workspace. Choose business for your own brand, or agency for your clients' brands, and type the business name. Now add your brand. Type the brand name. If customers spell your name differently, add those spellings too. Enter your website, sector and city, then tap add brand. Your brand page is ready. Report, recommendations, competitors and questions are in these tabs. In the next video, we add your questions and competitors.
