Then there's the AI that doesn't search the web. We asked Gemini the same questions without live search, so it answered from memory only. The result is striking. In smart rings, RingConn was recommended in thirty-five of forty-eight live-search answers. Gemini recommended it in just two of eleven. In Seattle it was the reverse: Starbucks, which fell behind with live search, was mentioned by Gemini in ten of fourteen answers. AI that answers from memory learns about new, fast-growing brands late, and it favors big, well-known names. Content published today only reaches these models with their next training.
