What about brands' own websites? For consumer products, only six of every one hundred sources were sites with the brand's name in the domain. Phones, TVs, coffee, smart rings: it's the same story. Whatever a brand writes on its own website, AI mostly reads what others write. Software tools are different. There, the share rises to about one in four. Semrush's own website alone was cited in forty-six answers. That's because software companies publish detailed guides that answer the exact questions customers ask. Most consumer brands don't.
