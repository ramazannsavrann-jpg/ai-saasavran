Pattern one: AI answering from memory and AI searching the web don't recommend the same brands. Our interpretation: fame lives in memory, not in today's reviews. An AI answering from memory, like Gemini, remembers brands people have talked about for years. That may explain why it recommended Nespresso in thirteen of sixteen answers. But when ChatGPT and Claude search the web, they read what today's review sites say. If Nespresso isn't at the top of current best coffee machine lists, you'd expect AI not to recommend it. A brand's past fame does not automatically turn into recommendations in live-search AI.
