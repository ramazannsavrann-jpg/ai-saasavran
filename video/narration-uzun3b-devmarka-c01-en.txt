Nespresso, Armani, Philips. Everyone knows these brands. But when you ask AI for a coffee machine, a perfume or a robot vacuum, do they get recommended? We measured it. ChatGPT recommended Nespresso in zero of sixteen coffee machine answers. Armani was recommended in none of thirty-two live-search perfume answers. Philips scored zero out of twenty-four on robot vacuums. In this video we look at four patterns in the data, the possible reasons, and how to see this for your own brand.
