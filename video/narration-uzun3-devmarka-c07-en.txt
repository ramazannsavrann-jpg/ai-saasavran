Pattern four: change the question, and the brand drops. Oura is one of the most recommended smart rings in the US. But when we asked for the ring with the longest battery life, it was recommended in zero of six answers. For a smart ring without a subscription, also zero, because Oura charges a monthly fee. A brand can be well known overall, and still lose to another brand on the specific question a customer asks, about price, battery, subscription or location.
