Finding three: every category has its own referee. When we asked about TVs, RTINGS was the source in twenty-one of twenty-nine answers. For sneakers it was RunRepeat, and for gaming laptops, Tom's Hardware. Local businesses bring in completely different sites: for Seattle coffee shops, Wikipedia and Yelp, and for New York movers, MoveBuddha. For health questions in the US, official sources like the CDC were cited. So there is no single magic website. Which site matters depends entirely on your category. The first job for any brand is to find its category's referee.
