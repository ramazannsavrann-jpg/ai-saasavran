The sources AI reads. In a live search measurement, the AI reads some websites while answering and cites them as sources. The report lists these sites, starting with the most used. Owned-source share shows how much of the sources come from your own site, and how much from your competitors. In the who the sources support section, you see which brand was recommended in the answers where each site appeared. Source opportunities lists the sites in answers where a competitor was recommended and you weren't; getting featured there can raise your visibility.
