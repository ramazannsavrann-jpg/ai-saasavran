Add questions and competitors. On your brand page, open the questions tab. This is where you write the questions your customers would ask an AI assistant. Tap suggest questions, and AI drafts questions based on your sector and city. Pick the ready-made questions you like with the add button, or write your own. To see whether AI recommends you on its own, leave your brand name out of most questions. Then switch to the competitors tab. Type a competitor's name and tap add competitor. This way you'll see who gets recommended instead of you. In the next video, we run the first measurement.
