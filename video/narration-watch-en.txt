Which smartwatch does AI recommend for Black Friday? We asked ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity the same eight questions, with live web search from the US.
We measured five brands: Amazfit, Apple Watch, Garmin, Pixel Watch and Galaxy Watch.
Apple Watch was recommended in forty-two of forty-eight answers. Garmin came second. Amazfit, just seven.
But each AI picks differently. ChatGPT never recommended Amazfit. Claude did, five times.
Change the question, and the winner changes. For running, Pixel Watch got zero. For battery life, Garmin wins every time.
Did AI get the facts right? We checked seventy-two claims against Apple's official specs. Twenty-seven were correct, none were wrong. And on Google Shopping, the top result for smartwatch is Amazfit, the brand AI recommended least.
And where does AI look? Each line links a review site to the brands it helped recommend. Tech review sites, not brand websites, shape the answers.
