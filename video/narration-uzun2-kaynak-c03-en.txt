The first finding is very clear. The most-cited website is Tom's Guide, in one hundred forty answers. TechRadar is second, with ninety-seven. Third is RTINGS, which tests products in its own lab. Then come Forbes, the Turkish price comparison site Akakçe, Wired and Consumer Reports. Look closely at this list: there is not a single brand website in the top eight. They are all review, testing and comparison sites. So when AI recommends a product, it usually trusts what these sites say, not what the brand says about itself.
