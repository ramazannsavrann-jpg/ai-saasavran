Run the first measurement. At the top of your brand page there are two measurement buttons. A measurement without search shows what the AI model already knows about your brand. In a live search measurement, the AI searches the web before it answers, which is closer to what your customers see. When you tap a button, your questions are asked one by one and every answer is analyzed. You can follow its progress in the runs tab. When it finishes, the report fills in and you get your visibility score. A measurement also runs automatically every week, so you can see how things change over time.
