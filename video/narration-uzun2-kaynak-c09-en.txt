For local businesses, the picture changes completely. When we asked about coffee shops in Seattle, AI looked at Wikipedia, Yelp and city guides. Every one of the six answers that cited Yelp mentioned a cafe called Storyville. Starbucks, born in Seattle, showed up in only six of forty-eight answers. So for a local business, how you appear on Yelp, Google reviews and city guides directly decides whether AI recommends you. Even the world's best-known coffee brand fell behind in its own hometown.
