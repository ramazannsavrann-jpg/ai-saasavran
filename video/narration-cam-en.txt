Which home security camera does AI recommend for Black Friday? We asked ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity the same eight questions, with live web search from the US.
We measured five brands: Arlo, Eufy, Google Nest, Ring and Wyze.
Eufy came first, recommended in twenty-four of forty-eight answers. Ring and Wyze tied at nineteen. Arlo got fourteen.
But each AI picks differently. Claude recommended Wyze fifteen times. ChatGPT, just twice.
Change the question, and the winner changes. Without a subscription, Eufy wins and Ring gets zero. For night vision, Arlo leads.
Did AI get the facts right? We checked thirty-six claims against Ring's official pages. Five were correct, none were wrong. And on Google Shopping, the top result for home security camera is Arlo, the brand AI recommended least.
