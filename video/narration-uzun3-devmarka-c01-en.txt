Nespresso, Armani, Starbucks. Brands everyone knows. But when you ask AI about coffee machines, perfume or coffee shops, are these brands recommended? We measured it, and the answer is often no. ChatGPT did not recommend Nespresso in any of sixteen coffee machine answers. Armani was missing from all thirty-two live-search fragrance answers. And Starbucks showed up in only six of forty-eight answers in its own hometown, Seattle. In this video, we look at five patterns in the data, and their likely causes.
