First, how we measured. We picked six product categories: robot vacuums, coffee machines, sneakers, perfume, gaming laptops and TVs. For each category we wrote eight buying questions, and none of them named a brand. We asked the same questions for Turkey and for the global market. ChatGPT and Claude answered with live web search. Gemini answered without search, from its own knowledge. In total, we collected five hundred and forty answers. It's a small sample, but the differences between the engines are very clear.
